Community Choice Awards

Annual awards decided only by verified reviews — not by fees, sponsorship or editorial favourites.

Eligibility

  • At least 10 published reviews in the 12 months before the cut-off, with at least half labelled Verified.
  • No upheld manipulation findings during the period.
  • Ranked within category by our published score (see methodology).

Timeline

Reviews submitted through the year count toward the next edition. Winners are announced each January; the first edition will be announced once enough verified reviews exist to make results meaningful.

Badges

Winners receive a free badge they may display on their website. Badges link back to the live profile so anyone can check the evidence.

Nominate a business

Nominations tell us where to focus outreach. They do not count as votes.
