How a review gets published here

Every review is checked by a person before it appears. Businesses can't pay to hide or reorder reviews.

  1. Submitted

    A reviewer shares a first-hand experience and discloses any relationship or incentive.

  2. Screened

    We check for duplicates, conflicts of interest and policy breaches, and may ask for proof of use.

  3. Labelled

    Reviews carry clear labels such as Verified, Invited or Incentivized, so you can weigh them.

  4. Published

    The business can reply publicly. Anyone can report a review that breaks the rules.
