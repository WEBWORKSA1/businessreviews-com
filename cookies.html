Cookie policy

How this site uses cookies and similar technologies.

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Essential storage

We store your theme and cookie-notice choice in your browser's local storage. These are needed for the site to work as you expect.

Advertising cookies

Google AdSense and its partners may set cookies to show and measure ads, including personalised ads based on your browsing. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland these require your consent, collected through a Google-certified consent platform.

Embedded media

YouTube videos load in privacy-enhanced mode only after you press play.

Your choices

  • Clear or block cookies in your browser settings.
  • Manage Google ad personalisation at Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of participating vendors at aboutads.info.
