About BusinessReviews.com

We help people choose business software and service providers with honest reviews, clear guides and free tools.

Why we exist

Buying the wrong software or hiring the wrong provider is expensive: wasted licences, lost months and painful migrations. Yet much of the information buyers rely on is either vendor marketing or reviews that are hard to trust. BusinessReviews.com exists to make that research faster and more honest.

What we do

  • Moderated reviews from people with first-hand experience, labelled so you can weigh them.
  • Buyer's guides that explain what to look for, how pricing works and what to ask — written to our editorial policy.
  • Free tools for comparing vendors and managing reviews.
  • Free matching for buyers who want a shortlist.

How we make money

We are funded by display advertising (Google AdSense), optional paid tools for businesses, a provider lead program, labelled sponsorships and reader support. None of these can buy a better rating, remove a review or change what our guides say. Read the full disclosure.

Our commitments

  • Businesses can never pay to remove or reorder reviews.
  • Every sponsored placement is labelled.
  • We publish our moderation rules and scoring method.
  • We correct errors quickly and say so.

Get in touch

Questions, corrections or partnership ideas: contact us. For interest in this website, domain, sponsorship, advertising or partnership: contact the owner.
