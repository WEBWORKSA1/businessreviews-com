The headline price on a software pricing page is the beginning of the cost, not the end. Total cost of ownership (TCO) adds everything you will pay to buy, run and eventually leave a tool.

The components of TCO

CostWhat to ask
LicencesPer user, per seat bundle or usage-based? What counts as a user?
Tier upgradesWhich must-have features sit in a higher tier?
ImplementationIs onboarding included, optional or mandatory?
IntegrationsNative, paid connector or custom development?
Data migrationWho cleans and imports historical data?
TrainingInternal time plus any vendor training fees.
Admin timeHours per month to maintain users, fields and automations.
Renewal increasesIs there a cap on annual price rises?
ExitWhat does it cost to export data and switch?

Model growth honestly

If your team will double, so will per-seat costs — and you may cross into a higher tier. Model three years, with realistic headcount growth and a renewal increase assumption.

Where to negotiate

  • Cap renewal increases in the contract.
  • Ask for implementation credits on multi-year terms.
  • Negotiate seat ramps so you pay as you hire.
  • Confirm free data export at termination.
