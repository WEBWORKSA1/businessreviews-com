Disclaimer & disclosures

How we make money and what that means for what you read.

Last updated: October 30, 2026

How we make money

  • Display advertising through Google AdSense. Ads are labelled and selected by Google, not by our editors.
  • Business plans for optional tools such as widgets and review insights.
  • Lead program: providers may pay when a buyer who opted in is introduced to them.
  • Sponsorships, always labelled as Sponsored.
  • Reader support through voluntary donations.
  • Affiliate links: we do not currently use affiliate links. If we add them, the affected pages will say so.

What money can't buy

No payment can remove a review, change a rating or score, alter review order or change the conclusions of a guide.

Reviews disclaimer

Reviews are the opinions of individual reviewers and do not represent the views of BusinessReviews.com. We moderate reviews but cannot guarantee the accuracy of every statement.

General information

Content is for general information and is not professional advice. Vendor pricing and features change; verify with the vendor. Listing a vendor is not an endorsement.

External links and videos
