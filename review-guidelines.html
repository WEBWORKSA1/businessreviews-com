Review guidelines

The rules for reviewers and businesses that keep reviews here useful and fair. These also serve as our user-generated content policy.

For reviewers

You may review if

  • You have a genuine, first-hand experience with the business or product in the last 24 months.
  • You are not an employee, owner, contractor, relative or competitor of the business.
  • You disclose any incentive or invitation.

Not allowed

  • Fake, copied, purchased or AI-generated reviews.
  • Reviews written for, or on behalf of, someone else.
  • Hate speech, harassment, threats, sexual content or profanity aimed at individuals.
  • Personal data: phone numbers, home addresses, health or financial details of anyone.
  • Promotional content, links to competitors, or spam.
  • Allegations of criminal conduct presented as fact, or content that breaks the law.
  • Multiple reviews of the same experience.

For businesses

  • Invite all customers equally. Selectively asking only satisfied customers ("review gating") is not allowed.
  • Do not offer incentives conditioned on a positive review, and disclose any incentive you offer.
  • Do not write, buy or commission reviews of your own business or competitors.
  • Do not use threats, legal pressure or non-disparagement clauses to suppress reviews.
  • Reply publicly and respectfully. Do not reveal customers' private information.

Breaches may lead to removal of affected reviews, a public notice on the business profile and loss of plan benefits. Payment never buys removal.

Moderation and appeals

Every review is checked before publication. If we reject or remove a review, we tell the reviewer why. Reviewers and businesses can appeal once by contacting us; a different moderator decides the appeal.

Report a review

If a published review breaks these guidelines, report it below. Reports are reviewed within 5 business days.

Legal notices

To report content you believe is unlawful or infringes your rights, use the form above or contact us with the URL, the content and the legal basis for your request.
