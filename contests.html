Official rules (summary)

  1. No purchase necessary. A purchase does not improve your chances of winning.
  2. Eligibility: open to individuals who have reached the age of majority where they live. Employees of BusinessReviews.com, sponsors and their immediate families are not eligible. Void where prohibited.
  3. Judging: entries are judged by our editorial team on helpfulness, originality and clarity. For review contests, the star rating and sentiment play no part in judging.
  4. Disclosure: reviews entered into a contest are labelled "Contest entry" when published, in line with consumer-protection rules on incentivised reviews.
  5. Prizes: announced per round; non-transferable; winners may need to answer a skill-testing question where required by law (for example, in Canada).
  6. Winners are notified by email and must respond within 14 days, or an alternate is chosen.
  7. Privacy: entry data is used only to run the contest. See our privacy policy.
